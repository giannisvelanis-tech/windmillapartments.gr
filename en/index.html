Winter in Galaxidi

Snow on the mountains, calm on the gulf

In winter Galaxidi grows quiet. Mornings are often sunny, Parnassos turns white across the water, and some years the snow reaches the lemon trees on the estate. Drive up to ski or for coffee in Arachova, see Delphi without the crowds, and come home in the evening to a view of the sea.

The estate

Your neighbours have feathers

The estate is gated, with wide open space and a garden. Peacocks and geese wander freely, and children never forget them. It is the only property on the forest side of Galaxidi: quiet at night, with the sea a short walk away.

The area

A seafaring village, with Delphi up the road

Galaxidi is a village of sea captains, with old mansions, a picturesque harbour and tavernas on the water. Small beaches and coves for swimming are all around. In winter it is an ideal base for Delphi and for skiing on Mount Parnassos near Arachova, and every Clean Monday the village celebrates its famous flour war, the alevromoutzouroma.
