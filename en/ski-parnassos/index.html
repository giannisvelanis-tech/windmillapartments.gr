Winter on Parnassos

Skiing on Parnassos, coffee in Arachova

In winter Parnassos turns white across the water from us. From Galaxidi the road climbs through Itea to Delphi, Arachova and the ski centre. Ski in the morning, stroll Arachova in the afternoon, and come home in the evening to a view of the sea.

Season: usually mid-December to early April, depending on snow. The 2025–26 season closed on 19 April. Before you set off, check parnassos-ski.gr for opening status, snow and prices.

Skiers at the Parnassos Ski Centre. Photo: HelenaKal, Wikimedia Commons, licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

The ski centre

  • Two areas: Kellaria, with runs for every level, and Fterolakka, with steeper runs for experienced skiers.
  • 27 runs and a Snow Park, 17 lifts (including gondolas and 4 baby lifts for beginners and children). The highest point is Vakchos, at 2,250 m.
  • Equipment rental is available at Kellaria (1,750 and 1,950 m) and Fterolakka (1,840 m), and ski lessons are offered.
  • Lift pass: in 2025–26 a day pass was about €30–35. Prices for the new season are published on the official site.

Arachova

The clock-tower rock. Photo: photosiotas, Wikimedia Commons, licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

Arachova is built on the slopes of Parnassos, just past Delphi, and from there the road to Kellaria is about 22 km. Its landmark is the rock with the clock tower, which locals call "Ora" (the Hour). Next to it are the church of Agios Georgios and the Folklore Museum, and further down the paved Lakka square with its shops.

Before you leave, pick up formaela, the local semi-hard cheese that is best fried, plus hilopites pasta and trahanas, and have a look at the woven textiles.

Tips

  • Carry snow chains. After snowfall, the road to the ski centre may require them.
  • Weekends bring crowds from Athens. Leave early, or go on a weekday.
  • Delphi is on the way. In winter the site closes at 15:30, so it suits a day off the slopes.

Two apartments for ski groups

Two independent two-bedroom apartments, up to 6 guests each, with views over the gulf and free parking. In winter, €120 a night for the whole apartment, 2 nights minimum, when you book directly with us.
